# v0.0.51: bounded live integration of validated lean direct scanning

v050 passed the RTX 5060 proof/correctness gates and 24-round three-way
comparison. Medians: packed direct v049 503651.82 tiles/s, stream reset only
507653.51, lean 617970.66. Lean improved controlled search throughput 22.70%
and cleared the 3% elapsed-time adoption margin. These synthetic full-loop
search timings are not overall live throughput or accepted pool shares.

This release adds --lean-direct to the bounded live worker, selecting
new_lean_search(...,lean=True), one stream and 4096-tile windows. --cache-a is
required; packed-direct, resident-maps and two-streams are mutually exclusive
alternatives. All existing defaults/backends are retained. Per-window status
clearing uses the validated same-stream memset. Uniform target validation and
candidate decoding use the validated scalar path, with all guard checks and
independent CPU candidate verification preserved. No scanner math, kernel,
nonce/proof rule or GPU allocation layout change. Existing v048 readable PTX
and v039 GPU B commitment PTX are reused without compilation on the rig.

The installer verifies 19 dependency manifests/files, reads the CPU library pin
from verified v031 metadata, and requires completed v050 preflight: both variants,
eight transcript windows, sixteen CPU samples, 36 target/queue cases, 56 guard
fault checks, both fresh V3 proofs passed by the official CPU verifier, populated
partial queues, retained windows and stale rejection. Completed balanced timing,
lean preference with a finite >3% elapsed-time win, and exact measured driver
counts/bytes are required. Sparse zero-valued diagnostic fields are supported.
Bad/missing gates prevent downloads and live connection. Old GPU tests are not
repeated, and no toolkit or pip package is installed on the rig.

One pool connection receives for at most 300 seconds. Heartbeats/progress are
printed every 15 seconds. Actual share targets are retained; complete candidates
are independently CPU-checked before fresh proof construction. At most one fresh
share is submitted; no reconnect or share retry. Status reports are accounted
separately from correlated share verdicts. Assigned extranonce as worker nonce
remains a hypothesis until a genuine custom share is accepted.

Report: mining-result.json, backend tag lean-direct-one-stream-v051. Terminal
summary adds Lean direct windows, alongside searched tiles/nonces, stage timing,
joined-worker lifecycle and actual submission verdict counters. Cache/context
ownership, stale rejection and shutdown joins are preserved.

84 host tests pass, covering live backend selection/lean=True, full-nonce
rollover, a scripted candidate/proof/correlated verdict, status acknowledgement
without a share, joined cleanup, incompatible flags before networking, installer
proof/guard/timing/reset/driver gates, scalar decoder equivalence/malformed
queues, stream ordering, all guards and stale-work cursor protection. Scripted
pool tests use fake GPU/proof data; they do not establish genuine acceptance.
v051 live execution remains pending on the rig.

The existing find_login helper reads a locally saved independent-login capture.
No wallet, capture, private header, nonce, seed or proof is published. No miner
fee or third-party miner code. Pool endpoint remains pool.pearlhash.xyz:9000.
CPU XMRig, clocks, permanent service definitions and autostart are preserved.
The Termius wrapper schedules a 15-minute WildRig fallback before stopping
pearl.service, bounds execution/cleanup to 13 minutes and explicitly restarts
WildRig on success or failure. No continuous custom miner is installed.
