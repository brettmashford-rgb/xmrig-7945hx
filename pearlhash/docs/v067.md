# v067: sequential transcript emission and native warp XOR

Offline experiment; v066 remains the validated bounded live path. No live worker integration.

## Hypothesis

The v064/v066 packed-four kernel uses 96 registers/thread on the rig. Its epilogue gathers all four 16-word transcripts before hashing/emitting them. This experiment gathers, hashes and emits one transcript at a time, allowing each temporary transcript to die before gathering the next. Register reduction and speedup are hypotheses until the rig reports compiled resources and timings.

Three paths in one readable sm_120 PTX: unchanged `four_tile_packed_scan`, `serial_emit_scan`, and `serial_emit_redux_scan`. The third additionally replaces the five-shuffle XOR fold with native `redux.sync.xor.b32`, previously a 2.6% improvement in v063. Shared maps, job B ownership, fresh A caches, canonical quartet traversal and six MMA instructions per K iteration are unchanged. Every lane participates in each gather; a full warp synchronization follows each lane-zero emission. No CTA barrier is added.
